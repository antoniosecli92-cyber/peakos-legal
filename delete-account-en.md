# How to delete your WAGMI account

**Last updated**: September 19, 2026

*Leggi in [italiano](./delete-account).*

## From the app (recommended)

1. Open WAGMI and sign in to your account.
2. Go to **Settings → Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm when prompted — deletion is immediate and irreversible.

## If you can't access the app

Write to **support@wagmifit.it** stating that you want to delete your account. **The request must come from the email address associated with your WAGMI account** — this is how we verify it's really you asking, before deleting any data. We'll process the request within **30 days (usually much sooner)**.

## What gets deleted

Whether you delete from the app or request it by email, the following is deleted **permanently and immediately** (not just hidden or deactivated):

- Your profile: email, name, photo, bio, city/gym
- All your workouts, personal records, routines, measurements, and check-ins
- Posts, comments, likes you've published
- Photos and videos linked to your profile, workouts, and posts (including form-check thumbnails from the AI Coach)
- Messages and conversations (chats with other users, AI Coach history)
- Reports and blocks linked to your account
- Connections to external services (Strava, Sign in with Google/Apple) — the connection is also revoked on those services' side, where possible
- Any other personal data associated with your profile

**This action cannot be undone**: once confirmed, your data is no longer recoverable, by you or by us.

## Questions

For any questions before proceeding, write to **support@wagmifit.it**. For details on how we handle your data, see our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy-en).
